# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the Stellar Alerts community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concerns, please report it as soon as possible through one of the following channels:

- **Email**: [conduct@stellar-alerts.dev](mailto:conduct@stellar-alerts.dev) *(monitored by project maintainers)*
- **GitHub**: Open a [private security advisory](https://github.com/stellar-alerts-labs/stellar-alerts/security/advisories/new) if the matter is sensitive
- **Telegram**: Contact a maintainer directly via the [Stellar Alerts community chat](https://t.me/+uElHrnWMb180MWM0)

When reporting, please include:

1. Your contact information (if you are comfortable sharing it)
2. Names (real, nicknames, or pseudonyms) of any individuals involved
3. A description of the incident, including any relevant links or screenshots
4. Any additional context you believe is important

All reports will be handled with discretion. The identity of the reporter will not be disclosed to the subject of the complaint without explicit consent.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

### Maintainer Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for:

- **Clarifying standards**: Keeping this Code of Conduct and related guidelines up to date and clearly communicated
- **Consistent enforcement**: Applying these standards fairly and consistently across all community spaces
- **Timely response**: Acknowledging reports within **48 hours** and completing an initial review within **7 days**
- **Confidentiality**: Protecting the privacy and security of the reporter throughout the process
- **Corrective action**: Taking appropriate action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful — up to and including a permanent ban

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

---

## Enforcement Process

When a report is received, maintainers will follow this process:

### 1. Acknowledgement
The reporter receives a confirmation within 48 hours that the report has been received and is being reviewed.

### 2. Investigation
Maintainers gather context, review any relevant artifacts (messages, commits, issues), and may contact involved parties separately and confidentially.

### 3. Decision
Maintainers determine the appropriate response based on the severity and context of the violation. Possible outcomes include:

| Severity | Example Violation | Consequence |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|
| **1 — Correction** | Inappropriate language, minor unprofessional conduct | Private written warning; public apology may be requested |
| **2 — Warning** | Sustained or repeated inappropriate behavior | Formal warning with specified behavioral requirements; temporary restrictions on interaction |
| **3 — Temporary Ban** | Serious violation of community standards | Temporary ban from all community spaces (GitHub, Telegram, etc.) |
| **4 — Permanent Ban** | Harassment, threats, or pattern of violations | Permanent removal from all community spaces |

### 4. Communication
Both the reporter and the subject are informed of the outcome. Details shared with the subject will not include the reporter's identity unless consent is given.

### 5. Appeal
Any party may appeal a decision by contacting the maintainers at [conduct@stellar-alerts.dev](mailto:conduct@stellar-alerts.dev) with new information or context within **14 days** of the decision.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code reviews)
- The Stellar Alerts Telegram community chat
- Any official project communication channels (email, social media)

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces (e.g., speaking at a conference, posting on behalf of the project).
